# Changelog

All notable changes to SubsForge are documented here. This project adheres to
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) and
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Unified "Forge" design system: pressable 3D button, glass top bar, cards with
  morph, and a grid aligned to the viewport.
- Tools: translate, sync and generate subtitles, merge a subtitle into the video,
  convert ASS to SRT, AI voice dubbing, summary and chapters, burn-in subtitles,
  and voice cleanup.

### Changed
- Redesigned light theme with a warm pastel palette, consistent with the other apps.
